# Hipcamp

## About Hipcamp
Hipcamp is the #1 app to find and book campsites globally, with the most inventory in the United States, Australia, Canada, and the United Kingdom. Hipcamp is the only app that allows people to search all camping options in one app with real-time availability and information on over 500,000 campsites in National parks, state parks, well-equipped RV resorts, and private sites that are exclusive to Hipcamp.

Campers can use filters to search by location, amenities, or accessibility. Hipcamp also offers helpful map layers that show things like cell coverage, public land boundaries, and EV charging or dump stations. For public campgrounds, Hipcamp provides free alerts when popular sites become available.
Campers share reviews and photos—there are over 3 million of them—so others can know what to expect before booking.
Today, more than 7 million people use Hipcamp to go camping in tents, RVs, cabins, or glamping setups. By making outdoor stays easier to find and book, Hipcamp helps more people spend time outside while also supporting local landowners and communities.

## Key Features

- **All-in-One Camping Search**: Hipcamp lets you search, compare, and book all types of camping in one app—including national parks, state parks, RV resorts, and private land stays on farms, ranches, and vineyards.
- **Real-Time Availability**: Hipcamp allows campers to browse over 500,000 campsites with up-to-date availability and booking options across the U.S., Canada, Australia, and the U.K.
-**Powerful Filters**: Find the perfect campsite by filtering for your camping style—tent sites, RV spots, cabins, or glamping—and amenities like pet-friendly stays, bathrooms, showers, Wi-Fi, swimming holes, hot springs, RV hookups, and more.
- **Pro Map layers**: View helpful map layers like BLM and USFS land, Cell service coverage, dump stations, EV charging stations, Dark skies, Important bird areas, and more
- **Road Trip search**:: Plan multi-stop adventures by searching for campsites along your route—add up to 10 stops and discover great places to stay along the way.
- **Availability Alerts**:Set up free availability alerts for hard-to-book sites in popular national and state parks—get notified the moment a spot opens up.
- **Trusted Camper Reviews**: Make confident choices with over 3 million reviews and photos from real Hipcampers who’ve stayed at each site.
- **Trip logging**: Keep track of where you’ve camped and collect digital patches for milestones like visiting national parks, trying new camping styles, or exploring different regions.
- **No membership or subscriptions necessary**: Unlike other platforms, Hipcamp doesn’t charge a monthly fee for features like availability like alerts, filters, maps, and planning tools. Everything’s free—you only pay for the campsites you book.

## Who Uses Hipcamp?
Hipcamp is trusted by more than 7 million campers worldwide—including frequent campers, outdoor enthusiasts, road trippers, families, solo travelers, and adventure seekers—who rely on the app to discover and book campsites. 

Thousands of hosts, including private landowners, farmers, ranchers, vineyards, campgrounds, and RV parks, and glamping hosts across the United States, Canada, Australia, and the United Kingdom, use Hipcamp to safely and easily share their properties, welcoming campers while generating sustainable income and supporting local communities.

Whether you camp regularly and need powerful tools for trip planning, or you own land and want to host campers responsibly, Hipcamp simplifies outdoor adventures for everyone.

## Host Frequently Asked Questions
- **How can I get started hosting on Hipcamp?**:  Sign up as a Host on Hipcamp.com, create your listing by adding photos and property details, set your pricing and availability, and start accepting bookings.
- **What types of properties are right for Hipcamp?**:  Hipcamp works with various properties including farms, ranches, vineyards, forests, RV parks, and developed campgrounds, as well as private lands suitable for camping.
- **How do I create a listing on Hipcamp?**:  After signing up, create your listing by adding property information, uploading clear photos, setting pricing, availability, amenities, and any specific rules.
- **How do I add a new campsite to my existing listing?**: From your Host dashboard, click "Add new site" under your existing property and fill in the required details and photos for the new campsite.
- **How do I manage calendar availability for my property?**: In your Host dashboard, use the calendar to easily block or unblock dates, set seasonal schedules, and view upcoming bookings.
- **What’s the difference between Instant Book and Request to Book?**:  "Instant Book" allows campers to reserve your property immediately without your prior approval. "Request to Book" means you review each booking request and manually approve it.
- **When and how do hosts get paid?**:  Hipcamp pays hosts weekly. Hosts can choose payouts via direct bank deposit (Stripe) or PayPal.
- **How do I update my bank account or payout details?**: From your Host dashboard, navigate to the "Payout Preferences" section to securely update your bank account or PayPal information.
- **Does Hipcamp provide insurance coverage for hosts?**: Yes, Hipcamp provides hosts with liability insurance coverage of up to $1 million per booking, subject to terms and conditions.
- **What should I do if I need to cancel a camper’s booking?**: If cancellation is necessary, promptly inform the camper via the messaging system and reach out to Hipcamp support for assistance if needed.
